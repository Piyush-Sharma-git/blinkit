About the brand

Quick commerce,
made for everyday.

Blinkit is an Indian quick-commerce company that helps customers order groceries and everyday essentials through its app. Orders are prepared at nearby dark stores for rapid delivery.

This is an independent academic project and is not affiliated with Blinkit or Eternal Ltd.
From Grofers to Blinkit

A brief timeline

A few milestones in the company’s evolution.

  1. 2013

    Founded as Grofers

    Albinder Dhindsa and Saurabh Kumar founded the online grocery company.

  2. 2021

    Rebranded as Blinkit

    The company adopted the Blinkit name and focused on quick commerce.

  3. 2022

    Acquired by Zomato

    The acquisition by Zomato was completed in August.

  4. Today

    Part of Eternal Ltd

    Blinkit is part of Eternal Ltd, formerly known as Zomato Ltd.

Company background: Wikipedia, “Blinkit” (checked October 2026). Details may change over time.

The academic project

One portal.
Two perspectives.

This demonstration models how a quick-commerce sales operation and its customers could use one shared system.

01

Customer store

Browse products, manage a cart, place a simulated order and view order history.

02

Sales dashboard

Manage transactions, customers and stock, and review sales reports with role-based access.

03

Shared database

Customer orders and internal sales records are handled through the same database.

About the sample data

Product data is illustrative. Customers and orders are synthetic sample records, and payments are simulated. No real payments are collected.
