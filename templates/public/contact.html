Frequently asked

Are payments real?

No. UPI, card and checkout are simulated. The site never asks for real payment details.

Why is an item out of stock?

Available quantities are tracked in the project database and decrease when an order is placed.

Can I see another customer's orders?

No. A customer account can only view its own orders.
